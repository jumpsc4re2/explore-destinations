Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you go.

Do I need a visa?

Visa requirements vary by country and your nationality. Always check with the official embassy before booking.

What is the best time to travel?

It depends on the destination! Europe is great in summer, while the Maldives is best from November to April.

Is travel insurance necessary?

Yes, we highly recommend it to cover unexpected cancellations or medical emergencies.

Can I travel solo?

Absolutely! Many destinations like Tokyo and Bern are very safe and welcoming for solo travelers.

How can I book a trip?

We are currently an information portal. We recommend booking through trusted sites like Booking.com or Expedia.

Are these destinations family-friendly?

Most of them are! Paris, Tokyo, and Switzerland have plenty of activities for kids.
