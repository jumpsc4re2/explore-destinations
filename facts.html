Fun Travel Facts ✈️

Discover interesting facts about our beautiful world.

✈️ The shortest commercial flight in the world lasts just 90 seconds — between two islands in Scotland!
🗺️ There are over 195 countries in the world — each with its own unique culture and beauty.
🌋 Iceland has no mosquitoes at all.
🏝️ The Maldives is made up of over 1,000 small coral islands.
🚄 Japan’s bullet trains can reach speeds over 300 km/h and are almost never late.
🏰 Switzerland has more than 1,500 lakes — you’re never more than 10 miles from one.
🌅 The Sahara Desert is roughly the same size as the entire United States.
🗻 Mount Everest grows about 4 millimeters taller every year due to shifting tectonic plates.
🐘 Africa is the only continent that stretches across all four hemispheres.
🗽 The Statue of Liberty was a gift from France to the United States in 1886.
